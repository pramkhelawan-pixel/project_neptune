# Neptune Engineering Roadmap

**Project:** Neptune Marine Intelligence Platform

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Active

---

# Vision

Build the most trusted evidence-based fishing intelligence platform for the South African coastline.

Neptune combines marine conditions, structured fishing knowledge, environmental intelligence and explainable decision-support to help anglers make better fishing decisions.

---

# Mission

Enable every angler to answer five questions before leaving home:

1. Where should I fish?
2. What species should I target?
3. What bait or lure should I use?
4. When should I fish?
5. Why is Neptune recommending this?

---

# MVP Definition

Version 1.0 is complete when Neptune can provide reliable, explainable recommendations for selected KwaZulu-Natal surf fishing locations.

Each recommendation must include:

- Target species
- Recommended bait or lure
- Best fishing time
- Confidence score
- Explanation

---

# Engineering Principles

## Knowledge is the Product

The intellectual property of Neptune is:

- Structured knowledge
- Fishing rules
- Recommendation intelligence
- Explainable decision making

The application delivers these capabilities.

---

## Explainability First

Every recommendation must be understandable.

Users should always know why Neptune reached its conclusion.

---

## Evidence Before Assumption

Knowledge should distinguish between:

- Scientific evidence
- Fisheries regulations
- Experienced angling practice
- Neptune validated observations

---

## Clean Architecture

Every feature must follow the existing architecture.

- Presentation Layer
- Domain Layer
- Data Layer

Business logic belongs in the Domain Layer.

---

## One Improvement Per Sprint

Every engineering sprint must leave Neptune measurably better.
