Read the Conditions. Fish with Purpose.

PELAV is a South African fishing companion app that helps anglers understand fishing conditions and plan their fishing. It brings together marine, weather, tide, and moon/solunar information, a personal catch and fishing-session log, a map of fishing spots, and species recommendations, so you can prepare before you head out.

What PELAV Offers

PELAV provides informational planning tools, including a PELAV Readiness Score and species recommendations based on a fixed set of environmental factors. For Premium users, it also provides a Digital Fishing Licence Vault for storing a copy of your licence on your own device, and a Length-to-Weight calculator.

PELAV's conditions and recommendations are informational only and are not a guarantee of fishing success, marine safety, or the accuracy of any forecast. PELAV does not determine your legal eligibility to fish — you remain responsible for holding any required fishing licence or permit and for complying with all applicable regulations. Always check current, official sources before fishing.
